How offline reading works

  • Saved on this device only. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is re-hosted. The PDF you keep is written into this browser's own storage by the service worker.
  • No expiry. A kept note is a plain file, so it opens whether or not the site — or Physics Wallah — is reachable.
  • It reads here. Open shows the PDF in this site's reader, out of this browser's storage. Save file puts a copy in your Downloads folder to carry elsewhere.
  • Kept is kept. A note is only marked kept once the file is actually in storage, so the badge does not lie about a download that failed.
  • Lecture video cannot be kept. Physics Wallah signs stream links per session and only for signed-in accounts, so there are no bytes here to save. The notes and DPPs are the part that works offline, and they are the part most students revise from.
